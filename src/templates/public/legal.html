1. Multi-Tenant Architectural Segregation

FieldOps Nexus enforces strict tenant-isolated data protection compliant with enterprise standards. Each organizational entity maintains its own isolated tenant context. Under no circumstances can data, documents, parts catalogs, or work orders be queried or modified cross-tenant without cryptographically verified authorization grants.

2. Transactional Audit Logging

All system mutations, permission role assignments, dispatch changes, parts issuances, and checklist completions produce immutable, append-only audit records. These records capture the actor identity, IP address, timestamp, and target entity metadata solely for accountability, auditability, and regulatory compliance.

3. Evaluation Gate Acceptance

This enterprise software is subject to a formal 90-calendar-day evaluation and acceptance gate. Operational data collected during active field operations is stored with continuous backup retention and protected by industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest.

ENTERPRISE PLATFORM FAQ

Frequently Asked Operational Questions

Q:

How does FieldOps Nexus integrate with existing ERP systems?

FieldOps Nexus provides bi-directional REST APIs and webhooks that synchronize inventory levels, purchase requests, asset cost ledgers, and workforce timesheets with SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, and Microsoft Dynamics.

Q:

Can field technicians work offline without internet connectivity?

Yes. The FieldOps mobile interface stores assigned work orders, asset documentation, and inspection forms locally. All signatures, photos, and status updates automatically sync when network access is restored.

Q:

How is Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) enforced?

FieldOps Nexus features granular role definitions for Super Admins, Asset Managers, Maintenance Planners, Technicians, Storekeepers, Auditors, and Clients, ensuring users only access verified data within their operational scope.

Q:

What level of audit traceability is maintained?

Key work order updates, status transitions, part allocations, signatures, and checklist entries are recorded in the audit log with timestamps and user identifiers.
